Hues & Cues

A digital, pass-and-play board for the color-guessing party game

Start a Game

How does this work?
Each round one player is the Cue Card holder. Only they get to see the secret target color on the board. They think of one clue word that describes it (e.g. "Ocean", "Rust", "Lemon") and say it out loud.

Everyone else places a guess peg on the numbered space they think matches that word. When everyone has guessed, the target is revealed. Guessers score points based on how close their peg is: 4 for an exact hit, 3 / 2 / 1 for each ring further out, 0 if too far.

The Cue Card holder scores 0 if either everyone scored or nobody scored — otherwise they score the same as the highest-scoring guesser (rewarding a clue that was good but not too easy). First to reach the target score wins.

This is a hotseat game — pass the device around, and look away from the screen when it's not your turn to see the target!
